# AUTHORS

## Origin

Ìlànà is built on lecture material from:

**SEN102/212 Software Engineering Process**
Department of Software Engineering
Obafemi Awolowo University, Ile-Ife, Nigeria

Lecturers whose material informs this work:

- Dr (Mrs) Onifade M. T. (requirements, design, interface, coding, testing, configuration management)
- F. C. Ugwu (process modeling and documentation, tools supporting software processes)
- Ubochi Chibueze Nmamouh (software quality assurance, process assessment and improvement, process
  ethics, team dynamics and industry practices)

Copyright in the original lecture material remains with its authors and with Obafemi Awolowo
University. The complete deck is included in `source/` for reference and attribution.
