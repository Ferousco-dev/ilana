# READING LIST

The sources behind Ìlànà, as cited in SEN102/212.

## Standards and bodies of knowledge

- **Pierre Bourque and Richard E. Fairley (eds).** *Guide to the Software Engineering Body of
  Knowledge (SWEBOK Guide V3.0).*
  IEEE Computer Society resources: https://www.computer.org/education/bodies-of-knowledge/software-engineering/resources/
- **ACM and IEEE Computer Society.** *Software Engineering Code of Ethics and Professional
  Practice.* https://www.acm.org/code-of-ethics
- **Carnegie Mellon University Software Engineering Institute.** CMMI and process improvement
  resources. https://resources.sei.cmu.edu
- **ISO/IEC 12207** Software Life Cycle Processes.
- **ISO/IEC 25010** Software Quality Model.
- **IEEE 830** Software Requirements Specification.
- **IEEE 1016** Software Design Description.
- **IEEE 829** Software Test Documentation.

## Process and methodology

- **Ken Schwaber and Jeff Sutherland.** *The Scrum Guide.*
  https://scrumguides.org/docs/scrumguide/v2020/2020-Scrum-Guide-US.pdf
- **The Scrum Guides Team.** *The Kanban Guide for Scrum Teams.*
  https://scrumguides.org/kanban-guide.html
- **Jez Humble and David Farley.** *Continuous Delivery: Reliable Software Releases through Build,
  Test, and Deployment Automation.*
- **Linux Foundation.** Introduction to DevOps learning resources.
  https://training.linuxfoundation.org

## Software engineering texts

- **Ian Sommerville.** *Software Engineering.*
  https://iansommerville.com/software-engineering-book/
- **Roger S. Pressman and Bruce R. Maxim.** *Software Engineering: A Practitioner's Approach.*
- **Rajib Mall.** *Fundamentals of Software Engineering.*

## Quality and process improvement

- **Daniel Galin.** *Software Quality Assurance: From Theory to Implementation.*
- **Watts S. Humphrey.** *Managing the Software Process.*
- **Robert C. Martin.** *Clean Code: A Handbook of Agile Software Craftsmanship.*

## Teams and people

- **Tom DeMarco and Timothy Lister.** *Peopleware: Productive Projects and Teams.*
  The primary industry reference for team dynamics: why cohesion, psychological safety and culture
  govern project velocity.
- **Frederick P. Brooks Jr.** *The Mythical Man-Month: Essays on Software Engineering.*
  Structural organisational breakdowns, communication overhead and developer synchronisation gaps.

## Ethics and society

- **Sara Baase.** *A Gift of Fire: Social, Legal, and Ethical Issues for Computing Technology.*
  Real-world case studies on confidentiality, intellectual property and accountability in system
  failures.

## Version control

- **Scott Chacon and Ben Straub.** *Pro Git.* https://git-scm.com/book/en/v2
