# PHASE 01 GATE

Full criteria: `gates/G1-requirements.md`.

Quick form for `RIGOUR` 1 to 2:

1. Every requirement has an ID.
2. Every requirement is testable.
3. Non-functional and domain requirements were considered, even if briefly.
4. Out of scope is written down.
5. A human other than the author has seen it.

If all five hold, pass and move on. Do not manufacture ceremony at low rigour; that is how
process gets a bad name.
