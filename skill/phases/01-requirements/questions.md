# PHASE 01 INTAKE QUESTIONS

Ask in one batch. Maximum nine. Choose by relevance, not by order.

## Always ask

1. Who are the users, and who are the stakeholders who are not users? Name roles, not job titles.
2. What is the single task this system must make possible that is currently impossible or painful?
3. What does the user do today instead? Walk through it step by step.
4. What must this system explicitly NOT do?
5. How will you know, three months after launch, whether this worked? Name a number.

## Ask when the domain suggests it

6. Which regulator, standard, or industry rule applies here? (health privacy, payment card,
   accounting, accessibility, data protection, sector-specific licensing)
7. What data does this hold, and what happens to a person if that data leaks?
8. What is the expected load: users at once, records total, transactions per day?
9. What is the availability expectation, and what does an hour of downtime cost?
10. What existing system must this integrate with, and who owns it?

## Ask when the project is a rescue or replacement

11. What does the current system get right that must be preserved?
12. Why did the previous attempt fail? Be specific.
13. Who has the authority to say "that requirement is wrong"?

## Ask when stakeholders conflict

14. If requirement A and requirement B cannot both be satisfied, who decides?
15. What is the deadline, and what happens if it is missed? (This tells you the real rigour.)

## Never ask

- "What tech stack do you want?" at this phase. That is phase 02, and asking it now invites
  the user to specify a solution before the problem is stated.
- Anything you can determine yourself by reading the repository.
