# PHASE 01 CHECKLIST

Tick honestly. An unticked box is a finding, not a failure of character.

## Elicitation
- [ ] Every stakeholder group is named, including the ones who will be affected but not consulted
- [ ] At least two elicitation techniques were used, not one
- [ ] The people who will actually operate the system were spoken to, not only the people who commission it
- [ ] Existing documents and the system being replaced were analysed
- [ ] Sessions are dated and attributed, so a requirement can be traced to a source

## Analysis
- [ ] Requirements are prioritised, not just listed
- [ ] Conflicts between requirements are identified
- [ ] Duplicates and overlaps are merged
- [ ] Feasibility assessed: technical and economic
- [ ] Risky requirements are flagged as `RSK-###`

## Specification
- [ ] Every requirement has a unique stable ID
- [ ] Every requirement expresses exactly one thing
- [ ] Functional, non-functional and domain requirements are separated and labelled
- [ ] Every non-functional requirement has a number and a unit
- [ ] Every requirement has an acceptance criterion you could hand to a tester
- [ ] Every requirement has a `source`
- [ ] Out of scope is written down explicitly

## Validation
- [ ] A stakeholder has read the SRS and confirmed it, with a date
- [ ] A test case can be derived from every requirement
- [ ] The adjective grep returns zero unresolved hits
- [ ] Models (data flow, ER, use case) reconcile with the written requirements

## Management
- [ ] The SRS is under version control
- [ ] A change control procedure is written down
- [ ] Traceability matrix exists and is populated
- [ ] Assumptions are logged as decisions, not buried in prose
