# PHASE 11 QUESTIONS

Maximum five, but ask questions 1 and 2 in every project regardless of mode.

1. **Who is harmed if this software is wrong?** Not inconvenienced: harmed. Name them.
2. **What data does this hold, and what happens to a person if it leaks?**
3. Who has the authority to stop a release on quality or safety grounds, and has that ever
   happened?
4. Who owns which component, and is that written down anywhere someone could check?
5. When two engineers disagree technically, how is it currently settled?

## Ask when choosing a process style

6. How stable are the requirements, honestly? Weekly change and quarterly change need different
   processes.
7. Is there a regulator, an auditor, or a contractual documentation obligation?
8. How available is the customer for feedback? Agile without customer availability is just
   short iterations.

## Ask when the team is distributed

9. What is the timezone spread, and which decisions currently wait for an overlap window?

## Ask when something has already gone wrong

10. Was this a knowledge problem, a communication problem, or a process problem? The three need
    different fixes and get confused constantly.
