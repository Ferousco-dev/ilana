# PHASE 11 CHECKLIST

## Ethics: honesty and integrity
- [ ] System capabilities described accurately, without exaggeration
- [ ] Known limitations documented and communicated
- [ ] Defects and risks reported truthfully, not softened
- [ ] Status reports reflect actual state, including bad news

## Ethics: confidentiality
- [ ] No personal, medical or financial data in fixtures, logs, examples or repositories
- [ ] Access to sensitive data is least-privilege
- [ ] Applicable data protection obligations identified
- [ ] Data retention and deletion defined

## Ethics: quality and safety
- [ ] Required standards identified and met
- [ ] Hazard or risk analysis performed where the system can cause harm
- [ ] No verification activity was skipped for speed or cost
- [ ] Known defects at release are disclosed, not concealed

## Ethics: intellectual property
- [ ] Every dependency's licence recorded and compatible
- [ ] No copied source whose licence forbids this use
- [ ] Attribution preserved
- [ ] No proprietary code from a previous employer

## Ethics: professional responsibility
- [ ] The team has the competence for this domain, or has said so and obtained support
- [ ] Established processes followed
- [ ] Accountability for outcomes is named

## Teams: roles
- [ ] Every role named with its responsibilities
- [ ] Every component has exactly one owner
- [ ] No two people unknowingly working on the same thing
- [ ] RACI exists for decisions that cross roles

## Teams: communication
- [ ] Requirements confirmed in writing with stakeholders
- [ ] A defined channel exists for raising defects and risks
- [ ] Handoffs between phases are explicit
- [ ] Distributed or asynchronous teams have a written-first norm

## Teams: trust and conflict
- [ ] People can report a defect without fear of blame
- [ ] Technical disagreements are resolved on agreed objective criteria
- [ ] An escalation path exists and is known

## Process style
- [ ] Style chosen deliberately: agile, plan-driven, hybrid, devops
- [ ] The choice is justified by project characteristics, not preference
- [ ] Regulatory constraints reflected in the choice
