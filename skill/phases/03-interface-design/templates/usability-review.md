## Observations

Record what the participant **did**, not what they said they would do. The gap between the two
is the finding.

| # | Observation | Principle affected | Severity | Proposed change |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | | structure / simplicity / visibility / feedback / tolerance | high / medium / low | |

Severity scale:
- **high**: task could not be completed, or was completed incorrectly without the user noticing
- **medium**: task completed but with a recoverable error or visible confusion
- **low**: friction, hesitation, or aesthetic complaint

## Note on sample size

One participant finds the obvious problems. Three to five find most of them. Reviewing with
zero participants and calling it a usability review is a G3 failure at RIGOUR 4 and above.
