# PHASE 07 QUESTIONS

Maximum five.

1. What does "good enough to ship" mean for this project, expressed as numbers? (This is the
   quality objective set, and most projects have never written it down.)
2. Which standard applies here: an organisational one, IEEE, ISO/IEC, a customer contract, or
   none? Name it.
3. Who is independent enough to audit this? An auditor from inside the delivery team is not an
   audit.
4. What quality data already exists: CI history, defect tracker, monitoring, review comments?
5. What quality problem has actually hurt this team before? (Prevention should be aimed at real
   history, not at a generic checklist.)

## Ask in regulated contexts

6. What evidence must be retained, in what form, and for how long?
7. Who signs off on quality, and what authority does that signature carry?
