# PHASE 07 GATE

Full criteria: `gates/G7-quality.md`.

Quick form for `RIGOUR` 1 to 2:

1. Somebody other than the author reviewed the requirements, the design and the code.
2. At least one number exists for each of: the product, the process, the project.
3. The seven quality attributes have been considered, with evidence or an honest `UNVERIFIED`.

At `RIGOUR` 1, "reviewed" can mean a conversation, recorded in one line. It cannot mean nothing,
because prevention is the whole point of the phase and review is its cheapest instrument.
