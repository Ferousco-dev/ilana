# PHASE 08 ANTI-PATTERNS

## Improvement Without Measurement
"We're going to improve code quality." No baseline, no target, no way to tell in three months
whether it happened. The course is explicit: without measurement, improvement is guesswork.
**Fix:** Article 10. Baseline, target, recheck date.

## Five Improvements at Once
Everything changes simultaneously, so when things get better or worse nobody knows which change
did it, and none of them are evaluated.
**Fix:** one change per cycle. Discipline yourself.

## The Retrospective With No Follow-Through
Held every sprint, actions written on a board, nothing carried into the next cycle. The team
has learned that the retrospective is theatre.
**Fix:** G8 criterion 5. One concrete change with an owner and a date, or the retrospective did
not happen.

## Claiming a CMMI Level
"We're CMMI level 3." No appraisal was performed. A formal level is awarded by certified
appraisers.
**Fix:** always say "indicative" and show the evidence.

## Confusing Level 3 With Level 4
Excellent documented, standardised processes, therefore level 4. But level 4 requires
quantitative and statistical control of process performance. Documentation alone is level 3.
**Fix:** the boundary test. Is any decision made from a statistical model of process performance?

## Metrics as Targets
Coverage becomes a target, so coverage goes up and quality does not. Defect count becomes a
target, so defects stop being reported.
**Fix:** metrics inform decisions; they do not become goals. Never attach individual performance
evaluation to defect data, or defect data stops being true.

## Optimising the Wrong Thing
Automating a step that takes two hours a month while the four-day review queue is untouched.
**Fix:** measure where time actually goes before choosing what to automate.

## The Process That Never Changes
Same process, same problems, every quarter, forever. Article 15 exists because of this.
**Fix:** a process with no change history is a process nobody is measuring.

## Assessment as Blame
Findings become an account of who failed. People start hiding data, and the measurement system
dies.
**Fix:** Article 14. Findings name missing mechanisms, never people.
