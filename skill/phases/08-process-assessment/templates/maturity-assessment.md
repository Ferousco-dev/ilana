### Boundary reasoning

State explicitly why the organisation sits at this level and not the next.

- *Level 2 to 3:* is there **one organisational process** that projects tailor from, or does each
  project define its own? Per-project documentation is still level 2.
- *Level 3 to 4:* is any decision made from a **statistical model of process performance**?
  Excellent documentation without quantitative control is level 3.
- *Level 4 to 5:* when did the process last change **because the data said it should**?
