# PHASE 04 GATE

Full criteria: `gates/G4-construction.md`.

Quick form for `RIGOUR` 1 to 2:

1. A formatter and a linter run, and pass.
2. No secrets in the repository or its history.
3. No commented-out dead code.
4. Someone other than the author has read it, even informally.

The secret scan is not optional at any rigour. Everything else on this gate can be relaxed;
that one cannot, because the cost of failure is unbounded and the check costs seconds.
