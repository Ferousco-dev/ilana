If a dependency's licence is copyleft and the project is proprietary, or the licence is unclear,
that is a finding, not a footnote. The source material treats using a protected library without
a proper licence or credit as a failure to respect intellectual property rights.

- A new dependency is added.

- A published advisory affects a version in use.
- Before every major release.
