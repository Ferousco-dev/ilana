# PHASE 10 QUESTIONS

Maximum five. Read the repository first.

1. What is already in use? (Check for CI config, board links in the README, tracker references in
   commit messages before asking.)
2. What does the team already know how to operate? Familiar and adequate beats unfamiliar and
   ideal.
3. What is the budget, and are there licences already paid for?
4. What must this integrate with: an existing tracker, an SSO provider, a corporate standard?
5. What manual step happens most often? That is the first automation candidate, and the answer is
   usually not the one people expect.

## Ask in regulated contexts

6. Must tooling be self-hosted, or is a hosted service acceptable?
7. What audit evidence must the toolchain produce, and for how long must it be retained?
