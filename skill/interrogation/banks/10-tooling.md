## Multiple choice

**Q1.** Which tool is most commonly used to automate CI/CD pipelines?
A. Jenkins  B. MySQL  C. Eclipse  D. Wireshark
*Answer: A*

**Q2.** Which tool is primarily designed for automated browser-based functional testing?
A. Docker  B. Selenium  C. Jenkins  D. GitLab
*Answer: B*

**Q3.** Upper CASE tools support which SDLC stages?
A. coding, testing, maintenance  B. planning, analysis, design
C. deployment and monitoring only  D. the whole lifecycle
*Answer: B*

**Q4.** In addition to storing product specifications and requirement documents, the central
repository of a CASE environment also serves as:
A. a version control system  B. a data dictionary  C. a test harness  D. a build server
*Answer: B*

**Q5.** A stated limitation of project management tools is that:
A. they cannot assign tasks  B. incorrect updates can lead to misleading reports and decisions
C. they always require self-hosting  D. they prevent risk management
*Answer: B*

**Q6.** A company implements task automation by incorporating CI/CD pipelines alongside automated
testing tools. What is the primary engineering goal of this optimisation technique?
A. to increase manual verification steps and expand the project timeline
B. to save time, eliminate human errors, and speed up the software development process
C. to replace the need for a documented Quality Management Plan
D. to ensure the project's actual cost remains higher than the planned cost
*Answer: B*

## Scenario

**S1.** Your team adopted Jira and configured fourteen workflow states with nine mandatory fields.
People now work outside the tool. Diagnose the problem and prescribe a fix.

**S2.** Your project board has not been updated in three weeks, but management still reports from
it. Explain the specific risk, citing the stated limitation of project management tools.

**S3.** You have two hours to automate something. The team performs a monthly two-hour manual
release step and waits an average of four days for code review. Which do you automate and why?

## Marking notes

- S1 must identify that the tool was configured beyond the real process, and prescribe matching the
  tool to the workflow rather than the reverse.
- S2 must cite that incorrect updates lead to misleading reports and decisions, making a stale board
  worse than no board.
- S3 should recognise that the four-day review queue dominates cycle time, and that measuring where
  time actually goes precedes choosing what to automate.
