## Multiple choice

**Q1.** Under CMMI, what is the fundamental prerequisite for transitioning from Level 3 (Defined)
to Level 4 (Quantitatively Managed)?
A. replace all organizational processes with ad-hoc individual heroics
B. begin managing, measuring and controlling process performance using quantitative data and
   statistical techniques
C. eliminate the product metrics phase to focus entirely on schedule adherence
D. achieve a state where development tasks are completed without any formal documentation
*Answer: B*

**Q2.** An appraiser finds that work is executed in an ad-hoc, unstructured manner, proper planning
is absent, and quality relies entirely on individual effort. Which maturity level?
A. 1 Initial  B. 2 Managed  C. 3 Defined  D. 4 Quantitatively Managed
*Answer: A*

**Q3.** An organisation has fully standardised and documented its engineering procedures across all
departments. Every team follows the same established processes. However, the firm does not use
statistical models or quantitative data to control process performance. Which level?
A. 1  B. 2  C. 3  D. 4
*Answer: C*

**Q4.** An appraiser finds that basic project management constraints such as scheduling and project
tracking are functional, but processes are not uniform or standardised across the company. Which
level?
A. 1  B. 2  C. 3  D. 4
*Answer: B*

**Q5.** Under ISO/IEC 12207, where are activities like corporate training programmes, infrastructure
management and institutional process improvement categorised?
A. primary  B. supporting  C. organizational  D. optimization
*Answer: C*

**Q6.** Under ISO/IEC 12207, a team sets up code version controls, baseline tracking management and
change authorisation procedures. Which category?
A. primary  B. supporting  C. organizational  D. optimization
*Answer: B*

**Q7.** Within the taxonomy of ISO/IEC 12207, where is "Software Maintenance" categorised?
A. primary  B. supporting  C. organizational  D. optimization
*Answer: A*

**Q8.** Under ISO/IEC 12207, where are software verification, validation and quality assurance
categorised?
A. primary  B. supporting  C. organizational  D. optimization
*Answer: B*

**Q9.** A team implements the cyclical loop "Measure -> Analyze -> Improve -> Repeat". What is its
long-term structural purpose?
A. to keep the project baseline budget completely static
B. to support continuous Software Process Improvement and optimise efficiency over time
C. to reduce the total number of primary processes required
D. to eliminate the necessity of conducting formal inspections and audits
*Answer: B*

**Q10.** Why is process measurement an absolute structural requirement before running SPI
initiatives?
A. because collecting metrics data automatically fixes syntax errors in the compiler
B. because it provides objectivity, ensuring process changes are based on facts and real data
   rather than subjective guesswork
C. because it shifts project workflows from supporting into primary processes
D. because it guarantees the project's budget will remain static
*Answer: B*

## Scenario

**S1.** Your organisation documents its process carefully for each project, but each project
documents its own. Someone claims CMMI level 3. Assess the claim and state what would be needed.

**S2.** A team wants to improve. They propose five changes at once: pair programming, a new
tracker, mandatory 80% coverage, daily standups, and a redesigned branching strategy. What is wrong
with this plan and what would you propose instead?

**S3.** You are asked to assess process maturity for an organisation that keeps no data of any
kind. What is your finding, and what is your first recommendation?

## Marking notes

- S1 must identify the level 2 to 3 boundary: standardisation must be organisational, not
  per-project.
- S2 must identify that simultaneous changes cannot be evaluated. One change, with a baseline, a
  target and a recheck date.
- S3 must treat the absence of data as the primary finding, and recommend measurement before
  improvement. Without measurement, improvement is guesswork.
