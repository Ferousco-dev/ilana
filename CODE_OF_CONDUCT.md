# CODE OF CONDUCT

## The short version

Be the kind of colleague described in phase 11: clear about roles, honest in communication,
generous with knowledge, respectful in disagreement, and professional when resolving conflict.

That is not decoration. This project is *about* team dynamics. A contributor community that cannot
practise them has no business publishing guidance on them.

## Expected

- **Communicate clearly.** Say what you mean. Ambiguity is a defect in conversation as much as in
  requirements.
- **Disagree on the merits.** Agree the criteria before evaluating the options. Seniority, tenure
  and volume are not arguments.
- **Assume competence.** People asking basic questions are usually new, not careless.
- **Share knowledge.** A review comment that explains why is worth ten that say what.
- **Accept correction.** Being wrong in public is how engineering works.
- **Report problems honestly**, including your own.

## Not acceptable

- Personal attacks, insults, or derogatory comments.
- Harassment of any kind, public or private.
- Discrimination or exclusionary language on any basis.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sustained disruption of discussion.
- Deliberately submitting content designed to defeat a security or safety control.

## Scope

Applies in the repository, issues, pull requests, discussions, and any space where someone is
representing the project.

## Enforcement

Report to the maintainers via a private channel listed in `SUPPORT.md`. Reports are handled
confidentially. Retaliation against a good-faith reporter is itself a violation.

Responses, in escalating order:

1. **Private correction.** A clear explanation of what was wrong and why.
2. **Public correction.** Where the conduct was public and the record needs it.
3. **Temporary restriction.** From the repository or from specific discussions.
4. **Permanent ban.** For repeated or severe violations.

Maintainers who violate this code face the same consequences, applied by the other maintainers.
A code of conduct that exempts its enforcers is not a code of conduct.
